# ART BIBLE

## Identity
Night industrial diorama: quiet, tactile, slightly worn, readable at phone size. It should feel designed, not like a collage of generated assets.

## Visual language
- Dark blue-black warehouse background.
- Warm off-white labels.
- Muted industrial surfaces.
- Destination colors are accents, not full-screen neon.
- Rounded rectangles, thick mechanical lines, small status lamps.
- Motion has weight: short easing, tiny overshoot, no excessive particles.

## Alpha palette
The exact values live in `game/scripts/game_theme.gd`. Code is the canonical palette.

## Typography
Use Godot/system fallback for the prototype. Before public release choose one properly licensed family and ship it locally.

## Rules
- No photorealistic assets.
- No random icon packs.
- No emoji in production UI.
- No gradients unless they serve lighting/material.
- Every new asset must match the same edge thickness, perspective and palette.

## Personality
Text is terse and in-world:
- NIGHT SHIFT
- LINE 03
- SORTED 128
- 02:13
Avoid generic mobile-game copy like “Amazing!” or “Epic reward!”.
